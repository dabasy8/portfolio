Fertile City

Architecture — 2025 — Technion

Fertile City is a design research project addressing desertification along the Besor Stream in the northern Negev. Reading the site's erosion and drainage patterns as design drivers, the project proposes a water-retention suburb and a wind-diffusing strip to stabilize the soil and restore fertility to the region. A parallel fabrication process develops a soil-based building material — combining local soil, biochar, cellulose, and psyllium — cast and dried into structural components for the city's construction.

MyBriX

Architecture / Material Research — 2022

MyBriX is a material research project developing living, fungus-grown building blocks. Mycelium is cultivated under controlled protocols, combined with 3D-printed forms, and grown into bricks that are catalogued, tested, and assembled into walls — from lab jar to architectural element.

Cultivation

Growing the fungus — testing substrates, jars, and cultivation protocols, and tracking growth over time to find reliable conditions.

Parametric Catalog

Hareshet 17

Integrative Studio — 2024 — Revit / Lumion / Physical Modeling

An education center combining kindergartens, a library, an art gallery, and a children's psychology center. The program was translated into volumes and composed through physical modeling. The structure is reinforced concrete at ground level and steel from Level 1 upward. Cladding follows a gravitational logic — stone, Dekton, and milky glass, heavy and coarse at the base, light and delicate at the top. Detailed design was developed in Revit.

One and One Third

Parametric Studio — 2021

A building with an ecological façade designed to serve non-human stakeholders alongside its human users. Parametric tools and environmental simulations drive the façade's form, shaping it into habitat and infrastructure for the life around it.

Site & Analysis

Reading the site and its light conditions, and mapping which species each part of the façade can support.

If Alone, We Better Be Moving

Design Studio — Kiryat Yam

Set on the coast of Kiryat Yam, where wind is the strongest force on site, the design works with the wind rather than against it. Repetitive modules break apart and reconnect to form a dynamic architectural system, open and shifting in response to its environment.

WoodenWood

Collaborative Research — 2024 — Robotic Printing

In collaboration with Avi Cohen, Yuval Berger, Alon Nisan & Shany Barath — Disrupt.Design Lab, Technion

Wood waste repurposed into seating through robotic printing and traditional woodworking. A parametric model links stool design, printing toolpaths, and material properties — balancing ergonomics, function, and material distribution — and produces a woven "wooden textile" deposition. Published at CAADRIA 2024; Gold Prize, Design Educates Awards 2023.

About

I'm an architect and entrepreneur (M.Arch, Technion — specialization track in Computational Design and Manufacturing) working at the intersection of computational design, material research, and robotic fabrication. WoodenWood, a collaborative project with my partners at the Disrupt.Design Lab, received the Design Educates Gold Prize and the Fuorisalone Award, and was nominated for the Dezeen Awards.

Today I work as an architect at Bar Orian Architects while continuing research in additive manufacturing. I also served as a Captain in the Armored Corps, commanding soldiers and officers in active duty and reserve.
