Studio Design

Hareshet 17

Integrative Studio — [2024] — Revit / Lumion / Physical Modeling

An education center with kindergartens, a library, an art gallery, and a kids' psychology center. The program was translated into volumes, which were composed through a physical medium. The building structure is reinforced concrete at ground level, with a metal structural system in L01 and above. The cladding is made of stone, Dekton, and milky glass, assigned to levels in gravitational logic — heavy and coarse at the bottom, light and gentle at the top. Detailed design was done through Revit.

About

I'm an architect and entrepreneur (M.Sc., Technion) working at the intersection of computational design and robotic fabrication. My work has won the Design Educates Gold Prize (2023) and been nominated for the Dezeen and Dori Awards. I'm currently an architect at Bar Orian Architects, alongside being a researcher in the additive manufacturing field. I also served as a Captain in the Armored Corps, commanding soldiers and officers in active duty and reserve.
