About

I'm an architect and entrepreneur (M.Sc., Technion) working at the intersection of computational design and robotic fabrication. My work has won the Design Educates Gold Prize (2023) and been nominated for the Dezeen and Dori Awards. I'm currently CEO and co-founder of a stealth-mode startup building ML-based software for the construction industry, alongside practicing architecture at Bar Orian Architects. I also served as a Captain in the Armored Corps, commanding soldiers and officers in active duty and reserve.
